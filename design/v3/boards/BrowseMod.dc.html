About this mod

A full Victorian ballgown for Ellen with a layered skirt, lace gloves and a matching ribbon on the tail. Works in the overworld, combat and cutscenes.

Toggles: Alt 1 gloves on or off, Alt 2 cycles three ribbon colours.

Text and images come from the GameBanana page.
