Notifications

One card for everything. The icon, colour, body, actions and progress bar are all optional, and the colour follows the app's rule: amber for updates and warnings, blue when something waits for you, red when something failed.

A notification about one mod shows its character, with the kind as a small badge on the portrait. One about several mods, or a mod with no character (UI, Unknown), keeps the plain icon.
